# Adobe 官方功能参考

仅在需要核对当前功能名称、菜单位置或能力边界时读取。Lightroom 与 Photoshop 会更新；以当前应用窗口和最新官方文档为准。

## Lightroom

- [Masking in Lightroom desktop](https://helpx.adobe.com/lightroom/desktop/edit-photos/masking.html)
  - Select Subject、Select Background、Select People
  - Add / Subtract 修边
  - Exposure、Highlights、Shadows、Temp、Tint、Hue、Saturation、Texture、Clarity 等局部调整
- [AI People masking and facial regions](https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/lightroom-cc/web/edit-photos/apply-masks/mask-with-ai.html)
  - Facial Skin、Body Skin、Eyebrows、Eye Sclera、Lips、Teeth、Hair、Clothes 等；可用区域以识别结果为准
- [Presets in Lightroom](https://helpx.adobe.com/lightroom/desktop/edit-photos/presets.html)
  - Portrait、Subject、Blur Background 等自适应预设和 Amount 强度
- [Lightroom vs Photoshop](https://www.adobe.com/mena_en/creativecloud/photography/lightroom-vs-photoshop.html)
  - Lightroom 适合非破坏性摄影调整和批量一致性；Photoshop 适合像素级精修
